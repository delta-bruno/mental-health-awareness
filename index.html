What is Mental Health?

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act in our daily lives.

Self-Care Tips

  • Get enough sleep
  • Exercise regularly
  • Talk to trusted people
  • Take breaks from screens
  • Practice mindfulness
