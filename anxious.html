Feeling Anxious?

Ground yourself and discover gentle calm.

Explore practical tools to feel safer, steadier, and more present.

Grounding Exercises

  • Notice 5 things you can see around you.
  • Touch a comforting item and describe it silently.
  • Take a slow walk and listen to your footsteps.
  • Focus on your breath for four calm counts.

Anxiety Coping Techniques

  • Speak kindly to yourself as you would a friend.
  • Accept tension without judging yourself for it.
  • Keep a small list of things that make you feel safe.
  • Plan one satisfying activity for later today.
